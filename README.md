## Section 1 – Overview

As a mother, I've seen firsthand that a one-size-fits-all education system doesn't work for every child. Each learner is unique, and education should adapt to their individual needs. Through this project, **AI for Education,** I share my experiments with various AI tools and models to understand their strengths and limitations. I firmly believe that when these tools are placed in the hands of parents, teachers, or educators, they should be easy to use and truly support the learning process without requiring hours of figuring out how they work.

---

## Section 2 – Purpose and Vision

- **Inclusive Learning:** Every child deserves an education that fits their individual needs.
- **Personalized Tools:** This repository offers resources to help educators and developers create products that adapt to each learner.
- **User-Friendly AI:** I've experimented with different AI tools and models to determine what works best. Tools should be straightforward so that parents, teachers, and educators can spend less time learning how to use them and more time helping children succeed.
