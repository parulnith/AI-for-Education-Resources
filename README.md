# AI for Education

Welcome to **AI for Education** – my personal project that brings together research papers, apps, tools, and experiments with AI to help make education more inclusive and tailored for every child.

## Overview

As a mother, I have seen firsthand how a one-size-fits-all education system does not work for every child. Every learner is unique, and education should be able to adjust to each child's needs. In this project, I share my experiments with various AI tools and models to understand their strengths and limitations. I firmly believe that when these tools are placed in the hands of parents, teachers, or educators, they should be simple to use and truly support the learning process without requiring hours of figuring out how they work.

## Purpose and Vision

- **Inclusive Learning:** Every child deserves an education that fits their individual needs.
- **Personalized Tools:** This repository is a collection of resources aimed at helping educators and developers build products that adapt to each learner.
- **User-Friendly AI:** I have experimented with different AI tools and models to determine what works best. Tools should be straightforward enough so that parents, teachers, and educators can spend less time learning how to use them and more time helping children succeed.

## Research Papers

- **GenAI-101: What Undergraduate Students Need to Know and Actually Know About Generative AI**  
  *Authors:* Sina Rismanchian, Eesha Tur Razia Babar, Shayan Doroudi  
  *Published:* January 2025  
  *Summary:* This study explores undergraduate students' understanding and perceptions of generative AI tools like ChatGPT. Findings indicate that while approximately 60% of students regularly use AI chatbots for academic tasks, many overestimate these tools' capabilities. Enhanced knowledge about generative AI correlates with more accurate assessments of its real-world applications. The research emphasizes the need for improved AI literacy to ensure effective and responsible use of these technologies.  
  *Link:* [https://doi.org/10.26300/1087-pv22](https://doi.org/10.26300/1087-pv22)

- **Education in the Era of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI): Understanding the Potential Benefits of ChatGPT in Promoting Teaching and Learning**  
  *Authors:* David Baidoo-Anu, Leticia Owusu Ansah  
  *Published:* January 2025  
  *Summary:* This paper examines how tools like ChatGPT can enhance teaching and learning experiences. It discusses applications in instructional materials, assessment, and professional development, highlighting the efficiency and differentiation benefits AI brings to education.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/education-era-generative-artificial-intelligence-ai-understanding-potential-benefits](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/education-era-generative-artificial-intelligence-ai-understanding-potential-benefits)

- **Does ChatGPT Enhance Student Learning? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Experimental Studies**  
  *Authors:* Ruiqi Deng, Maoli Jiang, Xinlu Yu, Yuyan Lu, Shasha Liu  
  *Published:* December 2024  
  *Summary:* This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluate the impact of ChatGPT on student learning outcomes. The study synthesizes experimental research findings to determine the effectiveness of ChatGPT as a learning tool in educational settings.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/does-chatgpt-enhance-student-learning-systematic-review-and-meta-analysis-experimental](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/does-chatgpt-enhance-student-learning-systematic-review-and-meta-analysis-experimental)

- **Analyzing the Impact of AI Tools on Student Study Habits and Academic Performance**  
  *Authors:* Ben Ward, Deepshikha Bhati, Fnu Neha, Angela Guercio  
  *Published:* December 2024  
  *Summary:* This research investigates how AI tools influence students' study habits and academic performance. It provides insights into the ways AI integration affects learning behaviors and outcomes, offering guidance for educators on effective AI tool implementation.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/analyzing-impact-ai-tools-student-study-habits-and-academic-performance](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/analyzing-impact-ai-tools-student-study-habits-and-academic-performance)

- **Scaffold or Crutch? Examining College Students’ Use and Views of Generative AI Tools for STEM Education**  
  *Authors:* Karen D. Wang, Zhangyang Wu, L'Nard Tufts II, Carl Wieman, Shima Salehi, Nick Haber  
  *Published:* December 2024  
  *Summary:* This study explores college students' utilization and perceptions of generative AI tools in STEM education. It examines whether these tools serve as supportive scaffolds or crutches that may hinder independent learning, providing valuable insights for educators and policymakers.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/scaffold-or-crutch-examining-college-students-use-and-views-generative-ai-tools-stem](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/scaffold-or-crutch-examining-college-students-use-and-views-generative-ai-tools-stem)

- **The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Students' Learning Experience**  
  *Authors:* Potter Kaledio, Abill Robert, Louis Frank  
  *Published:* February 2024  
  *Summary:* This paper provides an overview of how AI integration affects students' learning experiences, highlighting both benefits and potential challenges. It discusses personalized learning, immediate feedback mechanisms, and collaborative environments facilitated by AI, while also addressing concerns such as data privacy and the need for human oversight.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/impact-artificial-intelligence-students-learning-experience](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/impact-artificial-intelligence-students-learning-experience)

- **Application of ChatGPT in Higher Education and Research – A Futuristic Analysis**  
  *Authors:* P. S. Aithal, Shubhrajyotsna Aithal  
  *Published:* September 2023  
  *Summary:* This research explores the evolving role of advanced AI language models like ChatGPT in shaping the future of higher education and research. It anticipates the impact of such technologies on pedagogy, academic support, and scholarly inquiry, offering insights into potential benefits and challenges.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/application-chatgpt-higher-education-and-research-futuristic-analysis](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/application-chatgpt-higher-education-and-research-futuristic-analysis)

- **A Review of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Education During the Digital Era**  
  *Authors:* Pongsakorn Limna, Somporch Jakwatanatham, Sutithep Siripipattanakul, Pichart Kaewpuang, Patcharavadee Sriboonruang  
  *Published:* July 2022  
  *Summary:* This paper reviews the adoption of AI in learning and teaching during the digital era. It provides a narrative synthesis and systematic literature review, discussing the positive impacts of AI technologies on education, as well as associated risks such as safety, security, and privacy concerns.  
  *Link:* [https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/review-artificial-intelligence-ai-education-during-digital-era](https://scale.stanford.edu/genai/repository/review-artificial-intelligence-ai-education-during-digital-era)
