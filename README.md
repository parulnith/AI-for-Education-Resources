# AI for Education

Welcome to **AI for Education** – my personal project that brings together research papers, apps, tools, and experiments with AI to help make education more inclusive and tailored for every child.

## Overview

As a mother, I have seen firsthand how a one-size-fits-all education system does not work for every child. Every learner is unique, and education should be able to adjust to each child's needs. In this project, I share my experiments with various AI tools and models to understand their strengths and limitations. I firmly believe that when these tools are placed in the hands of parents, teachers, or educators, they should be simple to use and truly support the learning process without requiring hours of figuring out how they work.

## Purpose and Vision

- **Inclusive Learning:** Every child deserves an education that fits their individual needs.
- **Personalized Tools:** This repository is a collection of resources aimed at helping educators and developers build products that adapt to each learner.
- **User-Friendly AI:** I have experimented with different AI tools and models to determine what works best. Tools should be straightforward enough so that parents, teachers, and educators can spend less time learning how to use them and more time helping children succeed.
